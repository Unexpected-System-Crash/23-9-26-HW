 Obsidian starts off strong with a rather unique image which sets it apart from the millions of text files on my desktop. It's also a really neat note taking program that I have been meaning to properly use for some time now. 
 Unfortunately it is a bit difficult to use for a first time user, or at least it was for me. Though that could also be a skill issue on my part. 
 
 Final Review: 7/10
    -overall good but could use improvement.
