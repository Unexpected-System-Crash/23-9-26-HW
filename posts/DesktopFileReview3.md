Factorio, in spite of being the newest file of the three reviewed by a long shot, has far more going for it than the rest. This game is incredibly addicting. Its fairly simple to get into but just from what I have seen of people who have played far more than me, it can get REALLY complicated later. The thumbnail icon is also a neat 3d looking gear. 

Final Review: 9/10
    -This game will consume my soul and my free time.
